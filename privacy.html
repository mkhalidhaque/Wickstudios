Privacy Policy — Wick Studios

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Wick Studios is a private tool operated by Khalid Haque for uploading his own videos to his own TikTok accounts. It has no public users.

Data we collect

None from any member of the public. The app stores only the TikTok authorization tokens for the owner's own two accounts (@spot.the.scam and @i.let.ai.trade), which are required to upload drafts through TikTok's Content Posting API. No names, emails, locations, or device data of any other person are collected.

How data is used

The tokens are used solely to authenticate draft uploads to the owner's own accounts. They are not shared, sold, or used for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data retention and control

Tokens remain stored only while the app is in use. They can be revoked at any time from within TikTok's settings, which immediately ends the app's access.
